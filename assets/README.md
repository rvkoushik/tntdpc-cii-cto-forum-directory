# CTO Forum identity

The mark represents an open human centre surrounded by three connected signals: technology, leadership, and community. The open ring suggests inquiry rather than a closed system; the nodes turn that inquiry into collective intelligence.

## Assets

- `cto-forum-wordmark.svg` — primary horizontal lock-up for headers and documents.
- `cto-forum-wordmark.png` — 1240 × 264 px raster export for presentations and social use.
- `cto-forum-mark.svg` — compact mark for avatars, favicons, and small placements.
- `cto-forum-member-badge.svg` — member badge for the 2026–27 cohort.
- `cto-forum-member-badge.png` — 1024 × 1024 px member-ready raster badge.

## Usage

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the diameter of one outer node.
- Do not recolour individual elements or distort the artwork.
- Use the wordmark at 180 px wide or larger on screen.
- Use the member badge at 96 px or larger on screen.
- Core colours: Ink `#101820`, Signal Blue `#58B9DE`, Stewardship Gold `#D1A537`, White `#FFFFFF`.
